# Electronics

Breakdown of the electronics of the brush.

## Components specifications

|Component|Purpose|
|-|-| 
|Battery | Power the microcontroller|
|On/Off Switch| Turn On/Off the brush|
|Microcontroller| Handle the logic|
|LED|Indicate brush speed based on its intensity|
|Button|Change brush speed|
|Motor| Move the brush|

## Motor
Two options to consider for the motor:

1. **DC Motor** - Combined with a gearbox to convert rotational motion to linear motion.
2. **Vibration Motor** - Use vibration to move the brush.
